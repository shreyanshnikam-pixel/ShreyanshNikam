02 · About me

I build things that have to work when it matters.

I'm Shreyansh Nikam, a freshman at Livingston High School in New Jersey. I swim on the LHS team, help lead our school's Hack Club, and spend weekends at hackathons and game jams with a few friends. This fall I helped build Sky Sentry, an early-warning system that spots birds and drones near a runway, and Echo, a horror game where sound is the only way to see. Together they won four awards at two competitions, including first prize for best overall.
