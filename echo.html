How the game works

Sound is your only light

Echo is a first-person stealth-horror game made in Godot 4. You fall into an old mine called the Hollow, your lantern breaks, and the only way to see is to make noise.

Each ping sends out a ring of light that shows the walls for a moment. The Listeners are blind and hunt by sound, so every ping also tells them exactly where you are.

The theme was “Bounce Back.” An echo is sound bouncing back, and you are bouncing back from the fall by climbing out.

  • Sneak

    Move quietly through the dark.

  • Run

    Move fast when you have to.

  • Hold your breath

    Make no sound at all.

  • Throw pebbles

    Distract the monsters with noise somewhere else.

  • Whisper ping

    A quieter version of the ping.

  • Chalk marks

    Mark the walls as you go.

There is also an optional combat mode with hidden daggers.

What’s inside

  • Two levels, including The Flooded Deep
  • Cutscenes, a map and voice-log story pieces
  • A settings menu with key rebinding
  • Controller support and a ranking system

The story is told through voice logs you find in the dark. Audio from CC0 sound libraries, textures from Poly Haven, props from KayKit, and a custom 3D model for the monster.
